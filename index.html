G’Day! I am an Associate Professor in Project Management at the University of Technology Sydney, where I also hold a two-year appointment as Associate Professor, Graduate Research with the Graduate Research School. I work on how infrastructure gets delivered: how risk is allocated in public-private partnerships, what relationships do that contracts cannot, and how a project holds the consent of the people it is built among. The academic profiles listed here hold the record. This website is where I explain what it adds up to.
